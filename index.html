Biography

MT. Oung

Myoe Thant Oung (b. 1965)

Artist MT. Oung was born on 6 May, 1965 at Ahlone Township, Yangon. His mentors are Maung Maung Ba (U), Tin Maung Oo (U), WPM and very great master Ko Shwe Oung Thame.

He has participated in 60 group shows in Myanmar and also abroad.

His Solo shows in Myanmar are at Artist and Artisans Show Room in 1990, Korea Ambassador residence Myanmar in 1990, Myanmar Art Centre entitled the Strength of Life, Bahan Gallery (Myanmar Japon). His Solo shows in Singapore are at Dynasties Art Gallery in 1996, Orchard Point Exhibition Hall in 1997 and J Gallery, The Oriental in 2000, 2001, and 2003. He has participated in Wind of Artist and Residence at the Fukuoka Asian Art Museum in Japan, 2004.

His art works are collected in National Museum of Yangon, Myanmar and Fukuoka Asian Art Museum in Japan.
